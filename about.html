Common Questions

Are you a licensed electrician?

I handle technical trades and troubleshooting. For major panel upgrades or new home wiring, I work alongside or refer to licensed master electricians to ensure everything is to code.

Why focus on Starlink?

Living in the Ark-La-Tex, I know how vital good internet is. I specialize in Starlink because it's the best solution for our rural communities, and I ensure it's installed to survive our weather.

Do you travel?

Yes — how far depends on the job. For handyman work around Pittsburg, the $150 job minimum includes the first 40 round-trip miles; beyond that a trip fee applies. For Starlink installs I travel across TX, AR, LA, and OK — free within about 20 miles of Pittsburg, flat trip bands out to about 110 miles, and farther than that I quote the trip up front before booking.
