Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our 200-mile mobile service, Starlink installs, travel fees, and more.

Do you really travel a 200-mile radius?

Yes. Based in Pittsburg, TX (75686), we travel across East Texas and into Oklahoma, Arkansas, and Louisiana. We specialize in reaching rural locations and off-grid properties that standard local services won't cover.

How do your travel fees work?

The first 40 miles of the round trip are on me. Beyond that a trip fee applies. We utilize a mobile workshop to ensure all tools are on-site for remote jobs.

Do you provide the Starlink hardware?

No. You must order your kit directly from Starlink.com. However, if you use our referral link found on our Starlink page, you will receive your 2nd Month of Service for FREE.

Do you work on RVs?

Absolutely. We are experts in RV connectivity and maintenance. We specialize in Starlink Mini 12V DC power conversions and magnetic vehicle mounts for travelers who need reliable internet without a massive inverter.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major secure digital payments, including Square, Venmo, PayPal, and Cash App. Secure links are provided on our homepage and in invoices — pay after the job is completed to your satisfaction. (Note: A 3% fee applies to credit card payments.)
