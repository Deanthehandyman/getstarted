Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about my mobile service, Starlink installs, travel fees, and more.

How far do you travel?

Based in Pittsburg, TX (75686), I travel across East Texas and into Oklahoma, Arkansas, and Louisiana — I specialize in reaching rural locations and off-grid properties that standard local services won't cover. For Starlink installs: free within about 20 miles, flat trip bands out to about 110 miles, farther quoted up front. For handyman work, the $150 job minimum includes the first 40 round-trip miles, then $1 per mile.

How do your travel fees work?

For handyman work, the first 40 round-trip miles are included in the $150 job minimum — beyond that it's $1 per mile. Starlink installs use flat trip bands instead: free within about 20 miles, then published bands out to about 110 miles. I run a mobile workshop, so all tools are on-site for remote jobs.

Do you provide the Starlink hardware?

Either way works — it's your choice. You can order your kit directly from Starlink.com — use my referral link on the Starlink page and your 2nd Month of Service is FREE — or I can pick the kit up for you at Best Buy, Home Depot, or Walmart at that day's shelf price plus a $49 sourcing fee and bring it with me.

Do you work on RVs?

Absolutely. I specialize in RV connectivity and maintenance — including Starlink Mini 12V DC power conversions and magnetic vehicle mounts for travelers who need reliable internet without a massive inverter.

What payment methods do you accept?

I accept all major secure digital payments, including Square, Venmo, PayPal, and Cash App. Secure links are provided on my homepage and in invoices — pay after the job is completed to your satisfaction. (Note: A 3% processing fee applies to credit card payments.)
