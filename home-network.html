The Provider Gets You Online. I Make It Work Everywhere.

Out here, a "home network" isn't one router in an apartment. It's a long house with a metal roof, a shop a hundred feet out back, a barn past that, maybe a guest house or an RV pad by the driveway. The free router from the provider was never built for that — and neither was the house.

I design the network around your actual property: where the signal has to reach, what the walls and buildings are made of, and what you're trying to run on it. Then I install it, test it in the rooms and buildings that matter, and stay your call when something changes. I do the same work for Starlink customers — but you don't need a dish to hire me. If it plugs into your network, it's in my lane.

Network Services

Whole-Home Wi-Fi & Mesh

Mesh design and installation for any internet service — fiber, cable, or Starlink. Access points placed where your house actually needs them, so dead zones are gone from the back bedroom to the front porch.

Shop, Barn & Barndo Links

Point-to-point wireless links that carry your internet out to the shop, barn, barndominium, or guest RV pad — then proper coverage inside those metal buildings once it gets there.

Barndo & New-Build Networks

Building new? Get the network planned before the walls close. Wi-Fi planning, access-point placement, and hardwired drops for the office, TVs, and cameras — done once, done right.

Guest Wi-Fi for Rentals & RV Parks

Vacation rentals, Airbnbs, and lake houses around Lake Bob Sandlin live and die by their reviews — and guests mention bad Wi-Fi. Strong guest coverage, kept separate from your private network.

Small Business & Church Networks

Staff and guest networks kept separate, point-of-sale coverage that reaches the register and the back office, and Wi-Fi that holds up when the building fills up on Sunday morning.

Ethernet Drops & Camera Networks

Some things shouldn't ride Wi-Fi. Hardwired Ethernet drops for offices, TVs, and gaming, plus wired camera networks installed, configured, and tested end to end.

Not Sure What's Wrong? Start With a Network Checkup.

Buffering, dead rooms, cameras that drop offline, a shop with one bar of signal — sometimes you just need somebody to find the actual problem before you buy anything. A Network Checkup is a visit built for exactly that.

I map your speeds room to room and building to building, find the dead zones, and check your router placement, settings, and security. You get a straight answer on what's wrong and what it takes to fix it — with any fix work quoted itemized in writing before anything gets booked. No guessing, no parts cannon, no pressure.
