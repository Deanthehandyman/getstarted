Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you replace fascia boards after storm damage?

Yes. I remove and replace rotted or storm-damaged fascia to protect your roofline from further water intrusion.

Can you help stop bugs from getting into the house?

Yes. Sealing gaps in exterior trim and weatherstripping doors and windows is one of the most effective ways to keep pests out.

Do you do full siding or roofing jobs?

No, I focus on targeted exterior repairs like fascia, trim, and weatherstripping rather than full re-siding or roofing projects.

How fast can you get to storm damage?

I prioritize storm-related fascia and trim damage and can typically get out quickly — call or text (281) 917-9914 directly for urgent situations.
