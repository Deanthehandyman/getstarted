Frequently Asked Questions

Who uses your site assessment service?

Property managers, realtors, and insurance adjusters use my independent site assessments for unbiased, documented property condition reports.

What does a site assessment cost?

Assessments are a flat $150 with trip included in my regular service area. The full package with repair bids is $250, and large or distant rural properties run $300. Book online or call/text for a quote.

Am I obligated to hire you for repairs after the assessment?

No. The assessment is independent and no-bid — there's no obligation to use me for any follow-up work identified in the report.

How fast can I get a report back?

Turnaround depends on scope, but reports are typically delivered quickly after the on-site visit. Call/text (281) 917-9914 for current timing.
