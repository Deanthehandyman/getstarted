What I Usually Find

The dish got mounted in the easiest spot, not the clearest one — trees or the house itself blocking the signal. The router landed wherever the cable ran out, so speeds are great in one room and gone in the back of the house, with nothing reaching the shop or barn. Cable runs left loose, draped, or unsealed where they enter the house. And TVs, computers, and cameras that never actually got set up.

Common Questions About Starlink Setup

Can I hire someone to install my Starlink?

Yes! I specialize in local, professional Starlink installations. I handle the ladder work, the safe roof mounting, finding clear skies, and neatly hiding the cables so you don't have to lift a finger.

Do you provide the Starlink dish kit?

Either way works — it's your choice. You can order your kit directly from Starlink so the account is safely registered in your name — order through this link and get one month of Starlink free — or I can pick the kit up for you at Best Buy, Home Depot, or Walmart at that day's shelf price plus a $49 sourcing fee and bring it with me. Either way, I bring all the heavy-duty mounting hardware and cables and handle the installation.

How do I power the Starlink Mobile dish in my RV?

The Starlink Mobile dish is fantastic for travel. I can wire it right into your camper's 12-volt battery system. This saves a ton of power compared to running an inverter and plugging it into a standard wall outlet.
