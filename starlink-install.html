Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide the Starlink kit?

No, you must order your kit directly from Starlink.com to register the account in your name. Once you have it, I provide all mounting hardware, cables, and the professional installation.

What's different about mounting Gen 3?

Gen 3 lacks motors, meaning it must be precisely aligned manually to the northern sky. It also requires specific pipe adapters if you aren't using the kickstand. I handle the exact alignment mapping for you.

Can you power the Starlink Mini via 12V in my RV?

Absolutely. The Mini is incredibly power-efficient. I can bypass the standard AC brick and hardwire a DC-to-DC converter or high-wattage USB-C PD directly into your 12V battery bank for off-grid use.
