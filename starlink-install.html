Common Questions About Starlink Setup

Can I hire someone to install my Starlink?

Yes! We specialize in local, professional Starlink installations. We handle the ladder work, the safe roof mounting, finding clear skies, and neatly hiding the cables so you don't have to lift a finger.

Do you provide the Starlink dish kit?

No, you'll need to order your kit directly from Starlink so the account is safely registered in your name — order through this link and get one month of Starlink free. Once it arrives, just give us a call! We bring all the heavy-duty mounting hardware and cables to get it installed.

How do I power the Starlink Mobile dish in my RV?

The Starlink Mobile dish is fantastic for travel. We can wire it right into your camper's 12-volt battery system. This saves a ton of power compared to running an inverter and plugging it into a standard wall outlet.
