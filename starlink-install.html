Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide the Starlink dish?

No, you'll need to order your kit directly from Starlink.com so the account is safely registered in your name. Once it arrives, just give me a call! I bring all the heavy-duty mounting hardware and cables to get it installed.

What's different about the new Gen 3 dish?

The newest Gen 3 dishes don't have motors to move on their own anymore, meaning they have to be aimed by hand. I take the guesswork out of this by finding the exact right angle for the best speeds.

Can you make the Mini work on my RV battery?

Yes! The Starlink Mini is fantastic for travel. I can wire it right into your camper's 12-volt battery system, which saves a ton of power compared to plugging it into a standard wall outlet.
