Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a handyman install Starlink?

Yes. Dean is a specialized technical installer for Starlink Gen 3 and Mini, handling proper mounting, cabling, and Wi-Fi optimization for homes, metal buildings, and RVs.

Do you serve areas outside Pittsburg, TX?

Yes. We provide mobile Starlink and networking service across the Ark-La-Tex region, including Mt Pleasant, Tyler, Longview, Texarkana, Shreveport, and Broken Bow.

My Starlink is slow or keeps dropping. Can you help?

Absolutely. We troubleshoot obstruction issues, cable failures, and router problems, including on systems installed by someone else.

Can you get Wi-Fi into my metal shop or barn?

Yes. Metal buildings block Wi-Fi signal badly. We install access points and mesh extenders engineered specifically to push a usable signal through steel construction.
