What Does a Starlink Install Cost in East Texas?

Short answer: $350 for my base install, plus only the extras your place actually needs. Here's every line, in plain English, before you ever call.

The base install — $350

That's my labor to get you online, done right. The dish kit itself is separate — same as everywhere else — and the kit options are explained below.

What's included in the base install:

  • I find the spot with a clear view of the sky — trees and rooflines checked before anything goes up
  • Your dish mounted on your roof or a wall, anchored and sealed so it stays put in our weather
  • The cable run inside, neat and protected
  • Your router set up and your Wi-Fi tested where you actually use it
  • A walkthrough of the app before I leave, so you can see your speeds yourself

Half down books your day; the rest is due when the job's done and you're online. Your quote shows each line in writing before you book — nothing buried, nothing added later.

The add-ons — only if your place needs them

Most homes need one or two of these. Some need none. I tell you which before I start, and you approve the number first, always.

Mount provided by me

+$40

A proper roof or wall mount, supplied by me — and yours to keep.

Longer cable run

+$50

When the run needs more cable than the kit comes with.

Wall drop

+$75

Cable run inside the wall to a proper wall plate, instead of a cord coming through the wall or draped outside.

Tall pole / ground mount

Priced before I start

Pole and ground mounts depend on the height and the ground, so I price yours up front.

Extra Wi-Fi devices

Shelf price + $29, plus $50 setup per unit

Extra mesh router or access point for a big house, a shop, or a barn — I place it, pair it, and test your coverage before I leave.

The dish kit — two ways, your choice

Order your own: most folks order the kit straight from Starlink, so the account is safely registered in your name. It shows up, I install it.

Or I pick it up: I can get the kit at Best Buy, Home Depot, or Walmart at that day's shelf price plus a $49 sourcing fee and bring it with me. If the kit's $349 on the shelf, you pay $399 for it. If it's on sale that week, you get the sale price.

Trip fee — based on how far you are from Pittsburg

  • Local (up to ~20 mi) — no trip fee
  • Near (21–40 mi) — $40–$60
  • Mid (41–60 mi) — $60–$90
  • Far (61–80 mi) — $90–$120
  • Further (81–110 mi) — $120–$150
  • Beyond that — I still come. The trip price is figured up front before you book.

How that compares

For a typical comparison: a Starlink kit with an OnTech install typically totals about $548, and a kit with a Geek Squad install typically runs about $578–$628. Those are typical totals, not quotes — and they usually cover a basic install only.

With me, you buy the kit at retail (or I pick it up at shelf price + $49), my base install is $350, and every extra is printed on your quote before you book. You also get a local installer who checks your Wi-Fi coverage before leaving — and the same person answers the phone if you ever need help after. I also handle the jobs the big outfits usually won't: pole mounts, RVs, metal shops and barns, and whole-property Wi-Fi.

One more thing: some eligible Starlink plans may reimburse installation up to $350 — check your account to see if yours qualifies. If yours has one, I'll help you check and give you the paperwork to file it.

Quick Answers

How much is the base Starlink install?

$350. That covers mounting the dish, running the cable inside, setting up the router, testing everything, and showing you how the app works before I leave. The dish kit itself is separate — see the kit section below.

Do I have to buy the dish kit from you?

No. You can order your own kit from Starlink so the account is registered in your name. Or I can pick one up for you at Best Buy, Home Depot, or Walmart at that day's shelf price plus a $49 sourcing fee and bring it with me.

Is there a trip fee?

Within about 20 miles of Pittsburg, no — the trip is on me. Farther out I use flat bands: $40–$60 at 21–40 miles, $60–$90 at 41–60 miles, $90–$120 at 61–80 miles, and $120–$150 at 81–110 miles. Beyond that I still come — I just figure the trip price up front before you book.

Will Starlink pay for my install?

Some eligible Starlink plans may reimburse installation up to $350 — check your account to see if yours qualifies. If it does, I'll give you the paperwork you need to file it.
