Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do full bathroom remodels?

No. To keep scheduling fast and quality high, we focus on targeted fixture swaps and recaulking rather than full-room remodels, framing, or heavy concrete work.

How long does a shower recaulk take?

Most single showers or tubs are done in a couple of hours, including full removal of old caulk and proper cure time guidance.

Can you replace a kitchen or bathroom faucet same day?

In most cases yes, especially if it's a like-for-like swap. Book online or call/text to check current availability.

Do you serve areas outside Pittsburg, TX?

Yes, we provide mobile service across the Ark-La-Tex region, including Mt Pleasant, Tyler, Longview, Texarkana, and Shreveport.
